Pork Sisig
~50 mins

Sizzling, crispy, and bursting with flavor! Pork Sisig is a Filipino favorite, made with crunchy pork, zesty calamansi, and fiery chilies—served hot and sizzling. Perfect with rice or as the ultimate beer match!

Bistek Tagalog
~45 mins

Bistek Tagalog is a savory Filipino beef dish where thinly sliced beef is marinated in soy sauce and calamansi, then cooked with onions until tender. Making it a simple yet satisfying meal that’s always a hit.

Tinolang Manok
~50 mins

Tinola is a comforting Filipino chicken soup made with ginger, garlic, and onion, simmered with vegetables like papaya or chayote and fresh greens such as spinach or malunggay.
