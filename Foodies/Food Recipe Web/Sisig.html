PORK SISIG A sizzling Filipino favorite, Pork Sisig is made from tender and crispy pork, often seasoned with soy sauce, calamansi (or lemon), and spices for a perfect balance of savory, tangy, and spicy flavors. Traditionally served on a hot plate, it’s topped with a raw egg, which cooks on the sizzling surface, adding richness to the dish. Whether served as an appetizer or main course, Pork Sisig is packed with bold flavors that will leave you craving more.

INGREDIENTS

  • 500 g pork face or pork belly
  • 1/4 cup chicken liver (optional)
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 2 green chilies (chopped)
  • 2 red chilies (chopped)
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp calamansi or lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp mayonnaise (optional)
  • 1 raw egg (optional)
  • 2 tbsp butter or margarine
  • 1/2 cup water or pork broth
Sisig :>
Sooo good
Each bite delivers a mix of crunchy, chewy, and juicy textures, making it an exciting dish to enjoy with rice or as pulutan (bar chow). Its unique blend of flavors, along with that signature sizzle, makes Pork Sisig not just a meal—it’s an experience that captures the vibrant spirit of Filipino cuisine.

INSTRUCTIONS

  • Boil the pork in water with salt until soft
  • Boil in 30-40 mins, drain and let it cool.
  • Fry the pork until crispy, then chop into small pieces, set aside.
  • In a pan, heat butter, the cook garlic and onion.
  • Add chopped pork, liver and saute
  • Add soy sauce, calamansi juice, chilies, salt and papper, Stir well.
  • Add a little bit of water (or pork broth) to keep it moist.
  • Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed..
  • Put on a sisig platter, crack an egg on top.
  • Best served with rice.
  • Serve and Enjoy!

Its bold, sizzling charm makes it a standout on any table, and once you've had a taste, you'll understand why it's a true Filipino favorite.
