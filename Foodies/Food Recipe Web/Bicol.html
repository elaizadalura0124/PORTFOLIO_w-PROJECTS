BICOL EXPRESS Craving something bold and unforgettable? Bicol Express is a famous Filipino dish known for its creamy, spicy, and savory flavor. It originates from the Bicol region, which is known for its love of chili and coconut-based dishes. The dish is traditionally made with pork, coconut milk, shrimp paste (bagoong), and lots of chili peppers, making it a rich and flavorful dish with a spicy kick.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 kg pork belly (sliced into bite-sized pieces)
  • 5 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 Onion (chopped)
  • 2 Cups coconut milk (first extractions or "kakang gata" for a creamier texture)
  • 1 Cup coconut cream (for additional richness)
  • 2 Tbsp shrimp paste (bagoong alamang)
  • 5-10 Pieces chili peppers (siling haba or siling labuyo)
  • 1 Cup water (optional. for adjusting consistency)
  • 1 Tbsp cooking oil
  • Salt and Pepper to Taste

Bicol Express
Craving something bold and unforgettable? Bicol Express is the ultimate Filipino comfort food that packs a punch. Tender pork is simmered in rich, creamy coconut milk and infused with fiery chili peppers, creating a perfect balance of heat and flavor.

Each bite is a thrilling mix of spicy, savory, and slightly sweet—guaranteed to wake up your taste buds and leave you wanting more. Whether you're a fan of spicy dishes or just looking for something new and exciting, Bicol Express is a must-try. It's not just a meal—it’s an experience.

INSTRUCTIONS

  • Heat oil in a pan over medium heat
  • Sauté garlic and onion until fragrant
  • Add the pork belly and cook until it turns light brown.
  • Stir in shrimp paste (bagoong) and cook for about 2 minutes, allowing the flavors to blend.
  • Pour in the Coconut Milk and let it simmer for 15-20 minutes until the pork becomes tender.
  • Stir occasionally to prevent burning
  • Add the chili peppers and continue to simmer for another 5-10 minutes.
  • If you want it spicier, crush some of the chilies to release their heat.
  • Add the coconut cream and let it cook for another 5 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  • Stir occasionally to prevent the cream from curdling
  • Taste and season with salt and pepper as needed.
  • If the sauce is too thick, add a bit of water to adjust the consistency
  • Serve and Enjoy!
