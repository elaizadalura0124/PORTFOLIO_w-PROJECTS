TINOLANG MANOK Tinola is delicious because of its delicate balance of savory flavors from the chicken and aromatic ginger, enhanced by the freshness of the vegetables and the slight sweetness of the papaya or chayote. The warm broth, combined with the earthy greens, creates a soothing and satisfying meal that’s both nourishing and full of comforting taste.

INGREDIENTS

  • 500 g chicken (cut into pieces, bone-in preffered)
  • 1 thumb-sized ginger (sliced thinly)
  • 1 medium onion (sliced)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 medium papaya or chayote (peeled and sliced)
  • 1 bunch of malunggay leaves (Moringa)
  • 6 cups water
  • 1 tsp fish sauce (patis) or salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil

The simplicity of Tinola allows each ingredient to shine, with the tender chicken and aromatic broth complementing the crisp vegetables perfectly. It’s a dish that warms both the body and soul, making it a go-to comfort food for any occasion, especially when you're craving something light yet flavorful.

INSTRUCTIONS

  • In a large pot, heat 1 tbsp oil over medium heat.
  • Add ginger, onion, and garlic. Saute until fragrant and softened
  • Add the chicken pieces and cook until slightly golden.
  • Pour in water and bring it to a boil.
  • Lower the heat and let it simmer for 20-30 minutes, or until the chicken is fully cooked and tender.
  • Add the papaya (or chayote) and cook for about 5-7 minutes, until the vegetables are tender.
  • Season with fish sauce or salt to taste.
  • Add malunggay leaves and cook for another 1-2 minutes.
  • Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed..
  • Once everything is tender and flavorful, serve the Tinola hot with steamed rice.
  • Serve and Enjoy!
With its wholesome ingredients and rich, comforting flavors, Tinola is a dish that brings warmth to the table. Whether enjoyed during chilly weather or as a simple family meal, it’s a timeless Filipino favorite that never fails to satisfy.
