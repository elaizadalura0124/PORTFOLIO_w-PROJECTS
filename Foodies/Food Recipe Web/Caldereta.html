CHIKCEN CALDERETA A rich and savory Filipino dish, Chicken Caldereta features tender chicken simmered in a tomato-based sauce, flavored with soy sauce, liver spread, and a mix of aromatic spices. With added potatoes, carrots, and bell peppers, it’s a comforting, flavorful meal that’s perfect for family gatherings or special occasions. A true Filipino classic with a balance of savory, sweet, and tangy notes!

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 kg chicken (sliced into bite-sized pieces)
  • 4 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 medium onion (chopped)
  • 2 medium potatoes (cubed)
  • 1 medium carrot (cubed)
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 cup water or chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp liver spread (optional, but recommended)
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp fish sauce (patis) or salt to taste
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1-2 pcs bay leaves
Caldereta
:>
Perfectly balanced with a touch of liver spread for richness, and simmered to tender perfection, Chicken Caldereta is the kind of dish that warms you from the inside out. The combination of hearty vegetables and savory sauce makes it a satisfying meal that’s loved by many. Whether you’re serving it for a festive celebration or a cozy dinner, it’s guaranteed to be a hit on the table.

INSTRUCTIONS

  • Heat oil in a pan over medium heat
  • Sauté garlic and onion until fragrant
  • Add the chicken pieces and cook until browned on all sides
  • Stir in the liver spread and cook for 1-2 minutes, allowing it to dissolve into the sauce and create a rich base.
  • Pour in the tomato sauce and water (or chicken broth), then stir to combine.
  • Add the soy sauce, fish sauce (or salt), black pepper, and bay leaves. Mix well.
  • Cover and let it simmer for about 25-30 minutes, or until the chicken is tender.
  • Add the cubed potatoes and carrots, and cook until they’re tender and the sauce thickens.
  • Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed..
  • Best served with rice.
  • Serve and Enjoy!
With its comforting flavors and satisfying texture, Chicken Caldereta is more than just a meal—it’s a taste of home and tradition. Bring it to your next family gathering or enjoy it as a comforting weeknight dinner; either way, it’s sure to delight everyone at the table.
