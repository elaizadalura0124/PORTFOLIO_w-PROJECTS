DID YOU KNOW? French fries aren’t actually French! They’re believed to have originated in Belgium, where villagers used to fry small fish — but when rivers froze, they sliced and fried potatoes instead. So technically… they should be called Belgian fries!

LETS TALK FRIES


Fries are the crispy, golden treat that’s taken the world by storm — and honestly, who can resist? Whether they’re served in a paper cone at a food truck or piled high on a fancy plate at a restaurant, fries have earned their spot as the ultimate comfort food. With endless variations, from classic straight-cut to curly, waffle, and even loaded fries (hello, cheese and bacon!), there’s a style for everyone.


Fries aren’t just about being crispy — they’re about the experience. The thrill of dipping, the joy of sharing, and that unbeatable moment when you find the perfect fry in the bottom of the bag. Add a little salt, your favorite sauce, or go wild with toppings, and you've got yourself a snack (or meal, let's be real) that’s as fun as it is delicious.

NOW LETS GET COOKIN
Total Time 45 Minutes
Prep Time 45 Minutes
Cook Time 45 Minutes
Ingredients
  • 3 large potatoes
  • 1-3 cups oil
  • Salt
  • Paprika (Optional)
Instructions
  1. Peel and slice the potatoes into thin strips
  2. Soak them in cold water for 30 mins
  3. Dry and deep-fry until golden
  4. Drain, season, and serve hot
DOUBLE FRY, DOUBLE FUN!
Wanna know why some fries hit different? It’s all about double frying. First, you cook them low and slow to get that soft inside. Then you let them chill before tossing them back into hot oil — that’s when the crispiness kicks in. Crunchy outside, fluffy inside. Boom. Fry magic.
