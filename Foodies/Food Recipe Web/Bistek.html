BISTEK TAGALOG Bistek Tagalog is a classic Filipino dish made with tender beef marinated in soy sauce and calamansi, then cooked with caramelized onions. Its savory and tangy flavors make it a comforting meal, best served with steamed rice.
Cristyl Salva

~45 mins
4.8

INGREDIENTS

  • 500 g beef sirloin (thinly sliced)
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 2 onions (sliced into rings)
  • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup calamansi juice or lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp salt (to taste)
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper (to taste)
Bistek
Delicious!!
The combination of savory beef and sweet onions makes Bistek Tagalog a comforting dish that's both flavorful and filling. Often served with hot, fluffy rice, it’s a Filipino favorite that brings warmth and heart to any table. for a family meal or a special occasion, it never fails to impress.

INSTRUCTIONS

  • For marinating. In a bowl, combine soy sauce, calamansi juice (or lemon), minced garlic, salt, and black pepper.
  • Add the thinly sliced serloin into the mixture
  • Stir to make sure the beef is evenly coated with the marinade.
  • Cover the bowl and let the beef marinate for 20-30 minutes. You can marinate it longer for more flavor.
  • Heat 1 tbsp of oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  • Add the onion rings and saute until they are soft and slightly caramelized them.
  • One done, set the onions aside.
  • In the same pan, heat another tbsp of oil over medium heat
  • Add the marinated beef to the pan and cooked until brown and tender.
  • Add the sauteed onions back to the pan with the beef.
  • Pour in the remaining marinade and stir everything together.
  • Let it simmer for about 5 minutes until the sauce thickens
  • Serve the Bistek Tagalog hot, enjoy with steamed rice.
With its simple yet delicious flavors, Bistek Tagalog is a true Filipino classic that’s sure to satisfy your taste buds. Easy to prepare and perfect for any meal, it’s a dish that brings people together and makes every bite feel like home.
