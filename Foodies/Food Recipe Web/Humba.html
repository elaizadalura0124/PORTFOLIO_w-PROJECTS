PORK HUMBA Pork Humba is a sweet, savory, and slightly tangy Visayan braised pork dish in the Philippines. It is similar to Adobo but includes fermented black beans (tausi), brown sugar, and pineapple juice, giving it a rich and slightly caramelized flavor.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 kg pork belly (or pork hock for a gelatinous texture)
  • 5 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup pineapple juice (for sweetness & tenderness)
  • 1 tbsp fermented black beans (tausi)
  • 2 dried leaves
  • 1/2 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 1 cup water (adjust as needed)
  • 1 star anise (optional, for aroma)
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1/2 cup banana blossoms (optional)
Pork Humba
<3
If you haven’t tried Humba yet, you’re seriously missing out! Imagine tender, melt-in-your-mouth pork belly simmered in a rich, sweet-savory sauce, infused with the deep flavors of soy sauce, vinegar, and a hint of star anise. Every bite is a perfect balance of sweet, salty, and umami goodness, making it an absolute comfort food. Pair it with steaming white rice, and you’ve got a meal that’s pure heaven!

INSTRUCTIONS

  • Sear pork belly until golden brown on all sides. Remove and set aside
  • Sauté the Aromatics
  • In the same pot, sauté garlic and onion until fragrant.
  • Simmer the Pork
  • Add soy sauce, vinegar, pineapple juice, black beans, bay leaves, black peppercorns, and star anise.
  • Let it simmer WITHOUT stirring for 5 minutes to allow the vinegar to cook properly.
  • Add Water and Brown Sugar. Stir Gently
  • Cook and simmer for 45 mimutes to 1 hour, or until pork is tender.
  • Stir in Banana Blossoms and cook for another 5 minutes
  • Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper as needed
  • Let the sauce reduce until think and glossy.
  • Serve and Enjoy!
So, why do people love Pork Humba? Because it’s comfort food with a twist. Unlike adobo, humba leans into sweet-savory harmony, often with added banana blossoms or pineapple for extra layers of flavor. The use of tausi (fermented black beans) gives it a distinctive, earthy depth that sets it apart. Humba is perfect for celebrations—or any day you just need a warm, nostalgic meal.
